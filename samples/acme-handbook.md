# Acme Rockets — Customer Handbook

## About Acme
Acme Rockets was founded in 2009 in Tucson, Arizona by Wile E. Coyote. The company designs reusable model rockets for hobbyists, schools and research teams. Acme employs 48 people and ships to 31 countries.

## Products
The Roadrunner 1 is our entry-level rocket. It reaches an altitude of 300 metres, weighs 450 grams and costs $79.
The Mesa X is our mid-range rocket with a two-stage design. It reaches 1,200 metres, includes a GPS recovery beacon and costs $249.
The Canyon Pro is our flagship research rocket. It reaches 3,000 metres, carries payloads up to 500 grams, includes telemetry streaming and costs $899.

## Shipping and Returns
Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days and is free on orders over $150. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs $25.
Products can be returned within 30 days if unused. Rockets that have been launched can only be returned if they arrived defective. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of receiving the return.

## Warranty
All rockets carry a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. The Canyon Pro carries an extended 3-year warranty. The warranty does not cover crash damage, water damage, or modifications.

## Education Programme
Schools receive a 20% discount on orders of 10 or more rockets. Teachers can request a free curriculum pack with 12 lesson plans covering aerodynamics, Newton's laws and data analysis.

## Support
Support is available Monday to Friday, 8am–6pm Mountain Time, via support@acme-rockets.example or +1 520 555 0199. Average response time is under 4 hours.
